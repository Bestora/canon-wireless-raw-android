Pull RAW photos (CR3) off a Canon EOS camera over Wi-Fi and hand them to your editor. No Canon account, no cloud, no Canon app. Pair the camera once over Bluetooth; after that one tap wakes it, joins its Wi-Fi and shows the card.

<b>Features</b>

<ul>
<li>One-tap connect over Bluetooth: the app asks the camera to start its Wi-Fi and joins it</li>
<li>Gallery grouped by day, with a date scrubber to jump through thousands of photos</li>
<li>New shots appear live while connected</li>
<li>Filter by the star rating you set in-camera</li>
<li>Full-HD preview with pinch zoom</li>
<li>Full-resolution CR3, JPEG and HEIF download</li>
<li>Open in Luminar, Lightroom or any editor you pick once</li>
<li>Keeps the connection while you use other apps, reconnects by itself</li>
<li>Several cameras; EOS Utility mode by IP for cameras without Bluetooth</li>
</ul>

<b>Supported cameras</b>

Tested on the Canon EOS R5. The app speaks Canon's smartphone protocol, so other EOS bodies with Bluetooth likely work. If you try one, please open an issue with the result.

<b>Privacy</b>

No tracking, no analytics, no accounts. The app only talks to your camera.
